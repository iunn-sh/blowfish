0. 前言 Preamble
1. 隱私權對自由民主之不可或缺性 The Indispensability of Privacy to Freedom and Democracy
2. 最小化資料化權利 Right to Minimized Datafication
3. 非數位化之替代方案選擇權 Right to Meaningful Alternative
4. 促進民主自由之數位環境與公共心理健康維護 Promoting a Digital Environment Conducive to Democracy and Freedom and Maintaining Public Mental Health
5. 應重視程序與手段正當性 Ends Do Not Justify the Means
6. 政府開發或使用自動化系統（含人工智慧） Government Development or Use of Automated Systems (Including Artificial Intelligence)
